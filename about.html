Meet Sunita's Story

At 8 years old, Sunita had never been to school. Her family couldn't afford the fees, and she spent her days collecting firewood.

Today, thanks to our scholarship program, Sunita is top of her class and dreams of becoming a doctor to help her community.

"Education has given me wings to fly." - Sunita, Age 12
